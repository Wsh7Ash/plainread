# Deployment notes

It was decided by the committee that, although the implementation of the
aforementioned configuration mechanism was very arguably somewhat problematic in
several important respects, a comprehensive reconsideration of the underlying
assumptions should perhaps be undertaken by the relevant stakeholders prior to
any further deployment activities being initiated. A verification pass was
performed by the operations group and it was concluded by them that additional
documentation would be required by new team members who are very likely to be
confused by the current state of the runbooks and the various inconsistent
naming conventions that are still being used across environments which are being
maintained by different groups.
